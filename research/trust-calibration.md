# Trust Calibration

AI systems are often evaluated on accuracy, but effective collaboration
depends not only on accuracy, but also on calibration.

Calibration is the alignment between a model's confidence and the likelihood
that its answer is correct.

Research questions:

- How do humans calibrate trust?
- What cues increase or decrease trust?
- When should a model abstain rather than answer?
- How can users recover trust after an incorrect answer?
- How should calibration be measured?
- Can calibration be personalized for different users?

This document is intended as a living research note and will evolve as I read, experiment, and write.
